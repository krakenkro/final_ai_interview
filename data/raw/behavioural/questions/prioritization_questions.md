## Curated Question Set

1. Tell me about a time when you had too many urgent tasks at once.
2. Describe a situation where you had to choose between speed and quality.
3. Tell me about a time priorities changed suddenly. How did you respond?
4. Describe a case where you had to push back on scope or timeline.
5. Tell me about a time you made a prioritization decision that others disagreed with.

## Follow-up Prompts

- What criteria did you use?
- Who did you communicate with and how?
- What trade-off did you make explicit?
- Would you prioritize differently now?
