## Curated Question Set

1. Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate and how you resolved it.
2. Describe a case where you and another person had different technical opinions.
3. Tell me about a conflict with a stakeholder, manager, or client. What did you do?
4. Describe a time when communication broke down in a project and you had to repair it.
5. Tell me about a time you had to give difficult feedback.

## Follow-up Prompts

- What was the real root of the conflict?
- How did you keep the relationship productive?
- What trade-off did you accept to move forward?
- What did you learn about your own communication style?
