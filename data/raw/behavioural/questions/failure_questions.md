## Curated Question Set

1. Tell me about a mistake you made at work.
2. Describe a time when your plan failed. What happened next?
3. Tell me about a time you missed an expectation or deadline.
4. Describe a project that did not go as planned and what you learned from it.
5. Tell me about a piece of feedback that was hard to hear but useful.

## Follow-up Prompts

- How did you communicate the issue?
- What immediate recovery steps did you take?
- What changed in your process afterward?
- What part was your responsibility?
