## Curated Question Set

1. Tell me about a production issue or critical bug you had to handle.
2. Describe a time when debugging took longer than expected. How did you stay effective?
3. Tell me about a case where you had to communicate clearly during an incident.
4. Describe a situation where you found the root cause of a difficult problem.
5. Tell me about a time you improved the process after an incident or bug.

## Follow-up Prompts

- How did you prioritize mitigation versus root-cause analysis?
- Who did you keep informed and how often?
- What changed after the incident?
