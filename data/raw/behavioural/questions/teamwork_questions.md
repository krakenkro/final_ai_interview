## Curated Question Set

1. Tell me about a time you worked especially well with a teammate or cross-functional partner.
2. Describe a situation where team success mattered more than your personal preference.
3. Tell me about a time you helped unblock another person.
4. Describe a case where you had to adapt your communication style for the team.
5. Tell me about a time you contributed to a healthier team process or collaboration pattern.

## Follow-up Prompts

- What did you do specifically?
- How did your approach help the team outcome?
- What would have happened if you had not acted?
