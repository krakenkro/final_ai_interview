## Curated Question Set

1. Tell me about a time you took ownership of a problem that was not formally assigned to you.
2. Describe a situation where a task was at risk and you stepped in to keep delivery on track.
3. Tell me about a time you noticed a quality issue or hidden risk before others did. What did you do?
4. Describe a case where you had to coordinate with other people to finish something important.
5. Tell me about a time you made a mistake in your work. How did you handle the consequences?
6. Опиши решение, которое ты вел end-to-end. Какие компромиссы тебе пришлось принять?

## Follow-up Prompts

- What made you decide to act?
- What was your specific contribution versus the team's?
- What resistance did you face?
- What did you learn and what would you do differently now?

## Evaluation Hints

- Strong answers show initiative plus responsibility for the outcome.
- Weak answers sound heroic but vague, or avoid measurable impact.
- Good follow-ups probe action quality, accountability, and reflection.
