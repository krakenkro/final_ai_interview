## Follow-up Prompt Set

1. At what moment did you realize the plan was failing?
2. How did you communicate the problem?
3. What immediate corrective action did you take?
4. What part was truly under your control?
5. What process changed after that failure?
6. How did you avoid repeating the same mistake?
7. What does this story say about your judgment now?
