## Follow-up Prompt Set

1. What was the root cause of the disagreement?
2. How did you keep the conversation productive?
3. Where did you compromise and where did you not?
4. How did the other person likely see the situation?
5. What did you optimize for: speed, relationship, quality, or clarity?
6. What changed in your communication afterward?
7. What mistake would have escalated the conflict?
