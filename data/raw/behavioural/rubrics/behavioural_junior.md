## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Specificity

- `strong`: real example with enough context.
- `medium`: example exists but feels thin.
- `weak`: generic talking points without a story.

### 2. Ownership

- `strong`: candidate's actions are visible and honest.
- `medium`: some personal contribution is visible.
- `weak`: answer hides behind "we" or avoids responsibility.

### 3. Reflection

- `strong`: clearly explains learning or change in behavior.
- `medium`: some lesson is present.
- `weak`: no reflection or shallow takeaway.

### 4. Communication

- `strong`: easy to follow and reasonably structured.
- `medium`: understandable but loose.
- `weak`: confusing or overly abstract.

## Coaching Hints

- Junior candidates can be assessed on clarity and ownership even if stories are smaller in scope.
- Reward honest self-awareness and concrete actions over polished but empty answers.
