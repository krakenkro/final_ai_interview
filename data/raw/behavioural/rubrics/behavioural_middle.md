## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Specificity

- `strong`: concrete scenario with enough detail for follow-up questions.
- `medium`: partly specific, but some context is vague.
- `weak`: generic answer without a real story.

### 2. Ownership

- `strong`: personal role and actions are explicit.
- `medium`: candidate contribution is visible but mixed with team actions.
- `weak`: mostly "we", unclear responsibility.

### 3. Decision quality

- `strong`: explains reasoning, trade-offs, and constraints.
- `medium`: actions are plausible but reasoning is shallow.
- `weak`: no clear thought process.

### 4. Result and reflection

- `strong`: outcome is clear and learning is meaningful.
- `medium`: result exists, but reflection is weak.
- `weak`: no outcome or no lesson learned.

### 5. Communication

- `strong`: story is structured, concise, and easy to follow.
- `medium`: understandable but uneven.
- `weak`: scattered or confusing.

## Coaching Hints

- Ask the candidate to separate context from actions.
- Push for metrics, impact, or concrete outcome.
- Probe for what changed in their behavior afterward.
