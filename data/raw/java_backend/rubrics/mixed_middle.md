## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Technical reasoning

- `strong`: correct, practical, and trade-off-aware.
- `medium`: mostly correct but limited in depth.
- `weak`: oversimplified or partially wrong.

### 2. Ownership and delivery

- `strong`: explains decisions, constraints, incidents, and outcomes with clear responsibility.
- `medium`: involvement is real but end-to-end ownership is not always clear.
- `weak`: contribution is vague.

### 3. Systems awareness

- `strong`: discusses consistency, latency, failure modes, and maintainability.
- `medium`: some awareness, but mostly happy-path thinking.
- `weak`: little production awareness.

### 4. Communication and reflection

- `strong`: crisp, structured, and reflective.
- `medium`: understandable but uneven.
- `weak`: vague or defensive.

## Coaching Hints

- Ask how the candidate balances reliability, speed, and complexity.
- Mixed mode should surface both system judgment and personal maturity.
