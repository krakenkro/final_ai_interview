## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Technical base

- `strong`: understands basic backend concepts and can explain them simply.
- `medium`: partial understanding with some gaps.
- `weak`: incorrect or highly unstable understanding.

### 2. Story quality

- `strong`: gives concrete behavioural examples with clear role and result.
- `medium`: story exists but needs more ownership or detail.
- `weak`: vague and generic.

### 3. Reliability of reasoning

- `strong`: thinks carefully and avoids confident nonsense.
- `medium`: some good intuition, some guessing.
- `weak`: random or unsupported answers.

### 4. Growth and reflection

- `strong`: shows learning mindset and coachability.
- `medium`: some reflection but limited.
- `weak`: little ownership or learning signal.

## Coaching Hints

- Reward honest reasoning and concrete examples.
- In mixed mode, balance technical correctness with behavioural maturity.
