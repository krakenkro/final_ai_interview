## Follow-up Prompt Set

1. Where is the bottleneck here: JavaScript, layout, paint, hydration, or network?
2. What would you measure first before touching the code?
3. Why does this slowdown become visible to the user?
4. What is the cheapest change with the highest likely impact?
5. How would you avoid premature optimization here?
6. Which CSS, DOM, or rendering operation is most likely expensive?
7. How would you confirm the improvement after the fix?
