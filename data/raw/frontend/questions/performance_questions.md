## Curated Question Set

1. What happens between a React state update and the browser showing a new UI?
2. What is the difference between React render and DOM commit?
3. Why does a re-render not always mean a DOM update?
4. How would you diagnose a page that feels janky during interactions?
5. Why does main-thread blocking hurt user experience even if business logic is correct?
6. What is the browser's critical rendering path and why does it matter?
7. Why can some CSS properties be more expensive to animate than others?
8. Why are `transform` and `opacity` often preferred over layout-affecting properties in animations?
9. When is `requestAnimationFrame()` a better choice than `setInterval()`?
10. How would you explain the relationship between responsiveness, frame rate, and perceived performance?

## Follow-up Prompts

- What metric would you look at first?
- Is the bottleneck in JavaScript, layout, paint, or network?
- What would you change first without over-optimizing?
