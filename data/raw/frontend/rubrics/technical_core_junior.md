## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Fundamentals correctness

- `strong`: correctly explains core concepts like props, state, rendering, basic HTTP, or TypeScript basics.
- `medium`: mostly correct, but with small inaccuracies or missing detail.
- `weak`: confuses basic concepts or uses incorrect terminology.

### 2. Concept clarity

- `strong`: answer is simple, structured, and understandable.
- `medium`: understandable, but scattered or partially vague.
- `weak`: unclear, contradictory, or memorized without understanding.

### 3. Example quality

- `strong`: gives a small but relevant practical example.
- `medium`: example is generic or only partly connected to the concept.
- `weak`: no example or irrelevant example.

### 4. Learning readiness

- `strong`: shows coachability, awareness of gaps, and ability to reason forward.
- `medium`: can follow reasoning after hints.
- `weak`: rigid, purely memorized, or unable to adapt.

## Coaching Hints

- Reinforce vocabulary and clean mental models.
- Ask for tiny concrete examples instead of broad system design.
- Reward partial correctness if reasoning direction is healthy.
