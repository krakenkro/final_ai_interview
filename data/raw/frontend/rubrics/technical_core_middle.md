## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Technical correctness

- `strong`: answer is factually correct and terminology is used precisely.
- `medium`: mostly correct, but with mild imprecision or missing edge cases.
- `weak`: contains core misunderstandings.

### 2. Depth of reasoning

- `strong`: explains why a mechanism works and discusses trade-offs.
- `medium`: gives a surface explanation with limited reasoning.
- `weak`: gives memorized fragments without causal understanding.

### 3. Practicality

- `strong`: connects theory to production scenarios, debugging, or architecture decisions.
- `medium`: gives simple examples but limited production awareness.
- `weak`: no concrete example or relevance.

### 4. Communication

- `strong`: structured answer, clear flow, useful terminology.
- `medium`: understandable but scattered.
- `weak`: confusing, contradictory, or highly vague.

## Coaching Hints

- If correctness is weak, suggest core reading before deeper follow-ups.
- If depth is weak but correctness is acceptable, ask for examples and trade-offs.
- If communication is weak, ask for a step-by-step explanation.
