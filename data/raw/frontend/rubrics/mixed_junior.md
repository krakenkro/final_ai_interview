## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Technical fundamentals

- `strong`: basic frontend concepts are correct and usable.
- `medium`: some gaps, but core understanding exists.
- `weak`: major confusion in fundamentals.

### 2. Behavioural structure

- `strong`: gives clear story with situation, action, and result.
- `medium`: story exists but lacks detail or ownership.
- `weak`: abstract or generic answer.

### 3. Communication

- `strong`: calm, structured, easy to follow.
- `medium`: somewhat scattered but understandable.
- `weak`: hard to follow or highly vague.

### 4. Growth signal

- `strong`: reflects on mistakes and shows readiness to improve.
- `medium`: some reflection but shallow.
- `weak`: defensive or no reflection.

## Coaching Hints

- In mixed mode, do not over-penalize lack of depth if fundamentals are healthy.
- Ask for one concrete technical example and one concrete teamwork or ownership story.
