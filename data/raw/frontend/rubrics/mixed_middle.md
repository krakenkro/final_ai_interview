## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Technical depth

- `strong`: technically correct and explains compromises in frontend decisions.
- `medium`: mostly correct but surface-level.
- `weak`: shallow or partially incorrect.

### 2. Product and project ownership

- `strong`: clearly describes decisions, constraints, and end-to-end responsibility.
- `medium`: involvement is visible but not fully owned.
- `weak`: contribution and responsibility are unclear.

### 3. Debugging and reasoning

- `strong`: reasons step by step and can diagnose likely causes.
- `medium`: has intuition but limited systematic thinking.
- `weak`: jumps to guesses without explanation.

### 4. Communication and reflection

- `strong`: structured, concise, reflective, and specific.
- `medium`: mostly clear with some gaps.
- `weak`: vague, defensive, or weakly structured.

## Coaching Hints

- Probe for production examples, not only textbook definitions.
- In mixed mode, test both technical reasoning and behavioural maturity.
